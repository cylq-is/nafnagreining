is_words.txt.gz — Icelandic word forms

This file lists every word form generated from the Icelandic Hunspell dictionary
(is.dic and is.aff) of the hunspell-is project, by expanding its suffix rules
with tools/build_wordlist.py. The forms are unchanged; the file only lowercases
and sorts them.

Source:   hunspell-is, https://github.com/nifgraup/hunspell-is
          (as distributed in https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries, folder "is")
Based on: Íslenska Wikiorðabókin (Icelandic Wiktionary), https://is.wiktionary.org,
          and a public-domain word list by Orðabók Háskólans and Reiknistofnun
          Háskóla Íslands
